Wireless USB OC BigSurAdapter V17

Requirements: macOS Catalina or newer, OpenCore, SIP disabled (csrutil disable) and SecureBootModel set to Disabled.

What gets installed:

  • Status bar utility and helper into /Private/tmp (postinstall moves it into place)
  • LaunchAgents: Wlan.Software.plist and LaunchDaemon: Wlan.SoftwareDaemon.plist
  • Realtek kexts (RtWlanU, RtWlanU1827) copied to the ESP EFI/OC/Kexts folder and registered in config.plist Kernel > Add

Uninstall: choose the Uninstaller choice in the installer; it also removes the RtWlanU entries from the ESP config.plist.

Right-click the status bar icon and choose Enable/Disable or Open to control the adapter.
