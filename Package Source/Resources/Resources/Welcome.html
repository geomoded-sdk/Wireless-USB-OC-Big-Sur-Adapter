Wireless USB OC BigSurAdapter V17

Welcome to the Wireless USB OC BigSurAdapter installer.

This package installs the status bar utility, the launch agents and daemon, and the Realtek wireless kexts into your OpenCore EFI partition (Kernel > Add), enabling Realtek USB Wi-Fi adapters on macOS Catalina and newer.

Disable SIP and set SecureBootModel to Disabled in OpenCore before rebooting.
